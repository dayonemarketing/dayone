Search & GEO

GEO = Generative engine optimization

People now ask ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews and Perplexity which business to call. These tools write one answer and name a few businesses. GEO is the work behind getting your business named in those answers.

We pair GEO with local search, listings, structured data and paid ads, so you show up in Google results and in AI answers.

  • Google Business Profile
  • Schema and site structure
  • Google Ads
  • Meta ads and pixel tracking
